## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides insights into overall sales performance, customer activity, product categories, geographical performance, and sales team contribution.

The objective of the project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables users to quickly identify trends, compare regional performance, and analyze sales performance at different levels.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track important sales KPIs
- Compare sales across different regions
- Analyze product category performance
- Identify top-performing salespeople
- Monitor monthly sales trends
- Analyze sales team contribution
- Enable interactive filtering and drill-through analysis

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following KPIs:

- 💰 Total Sales
- 👥 Total Customers
- 📦 Total Boxes Sold
- 💵 Amount per Box
- 🧾 Total Orders

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Main Sales Dashboard

The main dashboard contains:

- KPI cards for overall sales performance
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Product Category
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Salespeople by Sales
- Salesperson Contribution Analysis

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically analyze the dashboard using filters for:

- Year & Month
- Quarter
- Product Category
- Region
- Sales Team

### Region Drill-Through

A dedicated drill-through page allows users to select a region and view more detailed information including:

- Regional Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Orders by Sales Team
- Product-level performance
- Salesperson performance
- Team contribution

---

## 🗂️ Data Model

The Power BI model follows a fact and dimension structure.

### Sales Table
Contains transactional sales information including:

- Sales Person
- Geography
- Product
- Date
- Amount
- Customers
- Boxes

### Products Table
Contains product information including:

- Product
- Category
- Size
- Cost per Box

### People Table
Contains salesperson information including:

- Sales Person
- Team
- Profile information

### Locations Table
Contains geographical information including:

- Geography
- Region

The tables are connected through relationships to allow cross-filtering and analysis across products, locations, and sales teams.

---

## 📐 DAX Measures

Custom DAX measures were created for calculations such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Customers
- Total Boxes
- Total Orders
- Amount per Box
- Sales Contribution

These measures enable dynamic calculations based on the filters selected by the user.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **3,791 sales transactions** covering:

- 25 Salespeople
- 22 Products
- 6 Countries
- Multiple product categories and sales teams

The data covers the period from **January 2021 to January 2022**.

---

## 🔎 Sample Insights

Some observations that can be derived from the dataset include:

- Total sales across the complete dataset exceed **$21.7 million**.
- More than **617K customer transactions/interactions** are represented.
- Approximately **1.34 million boxes** were sold.
- India, New Zealand, and the UK are among the strongest markets by sales.
- Sales performance can be compared across products, salespeople, teams, and regions.
- Monthly trend analysis helps identify changes in sales performance over time.
